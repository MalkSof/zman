Everything it does

Tracks by itself

Lives in the system tray and starts with your computer. When you step away, the clock stops.

Categories and a score

Apps and sites fall into categories with a productivity score from 0 to 100. Click any app or site to move it, and your whole history follows.

Focus mode

One click and distracting sites are blocked for 25, 50 or 90 minutes, with a countdown where the site used to be.

Daily limits and focus hours

"YouTube, 30 minutes a day." "No social media from 9 to 6." When the time runs out, the site is blocked until tomorrow.

Goals and gentle nudges

Set a daily goal of productive hours. zman tells you when you reach it, when you have drifted for a while, and how your week went.

Focus blocks

See the stretches of 25 minutes or more of real work, and what broke them.

Sites, not just "Chrome"

zman knows which site you are on and categorizes by site: YouTube is entertainment, GitHub is work. Nothing to install in the browser, and private windows are skipped.

All your devices

Optional sync to a server you host yourself with Docker. Computers and an Android phone in one place.

A terminal, if you like

zman today, zman week, zman export. The same reports with bars and colors, and your data as CSV.

Private by design

The code is open: you can check every one of these.

  • Never records keystrokes or what is on your screen.
  • Data lives in a file on your computer. No account needed.
  • Exclude any app, window title or site: it is never written to disk.
  • Private browser windows are never reported.
  • If you sync, it is to your own server, and window titles can stay home.

Questions people ask

Is zman free?

Yes. zman is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account, no trial and no paid plan: you download it and it works.

Is it an alternative to RescueTime?

Yes. Like RescueTime, zman tracks the apps and websites you use automatically and gives you a productivity score, categories and focus time. The difference is that zman is open source and keeps your data in a file on your own computer instead of in someone else's cloud.

How does it work with Claude and ChatGPT?

zman speaks MCP, the protocol AI assistants use to reach tools. Press Connect in the app and Claude Desktop can answer questions like "where did my week go?" from your real numbers. With your own zman server, ChatGPT and Claude on the web connect too. Assistants only receive totals, never window titles.

Can it block distracting websites?

Yes. Start a focus session to block entertainment and social media for 25, 50 or 90 minutes, set daily limits such as "YouTube, 30 minutes a day", or block sites during your working hours. Blocking uses the zman browser extension.

What does zman record? Is it private?

Only the name of the app in use, the window title, the address of the site in your browser and for how long. It never records keystrokes, screenshots or the content of pages, and it skips private browser windows. Nothing leaves your computer unless you sign in to a server you run yourself.

Which systems does it support?

Windows 10 and 11 today, with an Android companion app for phone usage. macOS and Linux are not supported yet.
